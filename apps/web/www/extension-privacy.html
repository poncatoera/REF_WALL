Wall browser extension: privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The Wall extension sends pictures, videos, links and screenshots you choose to your own Wall: the Wall app on your computer, or a Wall page you have open in a tab. It has no analytics and collects nothing about you for its makers. If you send to Wall on the web, the item is saved and synced through the Wall server like anything else you add to that board.

What it handles, and where it goes

  • Things you choose to add (a picture, a video, a link, a screenshot, and the page's address and title) go only to the Wall app on this computer, at 127.0.0.1, or to the Wall page open in your browser. To get a picture, the extension downloads it from the website it's on. Nothing else is read from pages.
  • Sign-ins for video sites. When you add a video or a link from Instagram, X, Vimeo, TikTok, Facebook, Threads or Patreon, the extension passes your browser's cookies for that one site to the Wall app on this computer, so it can download a video only signed-in people can see. They are used only to sign in to that same site for the download, are not sent anywhere else, and the app deletes them as soon as the download is done.
  • Checking for Wall. Once a minute, and when tabs load, the extension asks the Wall app on this computer (127.0.0.1) for its board list and looks for an open Wall page, to keep the right-click menu current. Nothing leaves your computer.
  • Instagram and X. On those sites a small script notes which post you right-clicked, so the post's link (not the whole feed) is sent to Wall. It reads nothing else.
  • Settings (which board you last sent to, clip start and end times) stay in your browser's extension storage.

Why it asks for its permissions

  • Read and change data on all websites: to fetch the picture you right-clicked as your browser sees it, take screenshots, read a video's current time for clip marks, and hand files to an open Wall page.
  • Cookies: only for the video sites listed above, only when you add a video or link from one of them, as described above.
  • Context menus, scripting, storage, alarms: the right-click menu, the area screenshot and clip times, remembering your choices, and keeping the board list current.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It doesn't track browsing or read pages you don't send from.
  • It doesn't use data for advertising, credit or anything other than adding things to your Wall.

If you share a Wall board online, what you added is stored with that board as you would expect; see the board's own sharing settings.
